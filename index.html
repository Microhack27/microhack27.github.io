OUR CENTRE

MICROCHIP – Centre for Edge and Embedded Intelligence

INAUGURATED ON 05 SEPTEMBER 2026

The MICROCHIP – Centre for Edge and Embedded Intelligence was inaugurated on 5th September 2026, marking an important milestone in promoting innovation, hands-on learning, embedded technologies, and edge intelligence. The centre provides students with opportunities to explore emerging technologies, conduct research, and develop innovative real-world projects. As part of its commitment to fostering innovation and technical excellence, the centre is proudly organizing MICROHACK'27, a National Level 36 Hours Hackathon.
